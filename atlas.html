Symbol Atlas

Every rune, trait glyph and skill sigil a sigil can draw, rendered at a fixed scale so shapes can be compared directly. Back to the sigils · Help

Runes

Script-like marks written around the rune bands and inside medallion rings, grouped by the tradition whose style each best fits. A spell writes only with runes from its own traditions; traditionless spells sample all four. Numbers are each rune's position in its tradition's list.

Trait glyphs

Drawn in the medallion at the center and in the medallions around the outer band. Each label is the trait it stands for.

Skill sigils

For the caster stations around a ritual. Each is shown large, then in a station square as a required check (solid) and as one of a choice (dotted). The four magical skills take their tradition's color. Every named Lore uses the Lore sigil, and a check that accepts any skill leaves its station empty.

Drawn live from js/symbols.js; each description is the comment beside its symbol there.
